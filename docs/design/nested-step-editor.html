MONKEYBOY’S Nested step editor storyboard Design study for issue #146

Panels use the measured sizes: SEQ 10 HP (120 px) with a 104 × 73 px editor, DRUMS 14 HP (168 px) with 109 × 18 px rows. This page simulates only. Its small parser, playhead and randomness stand in for @strudel/mini and the engine; it does not play sound or write a patch. See the study for the real parser evidence.

Keyboard

        

          
Tab

          
Enter or leave the editor: one stop per editor

          
← →

          
Previous or next cell at this level

          
↑ ↓

          
Line above or below, or the next lane

          
Enter

          
Open a group, or edit a note or text cell

          
Esc

          
Back out one level; cancels an edit

          
[ ]

          
Split the step, or merge a group

          
* / ! @

          
Add that modifier; press again to step its value

          
?

          
Toggle maybe

          
< , |

          
Add an alternative, chord note or random choice

          
~

          
Toggle rest

          
+

          
Add a step after this cell

          
⌫

          
Remove the last modifier, then the cell
